Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Overview

punchDev ("we," "us," or "our") operates punchdev.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and purchase our products.

We treat your data like production secrets—encrypted, secure, and handled with care.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information collected automatically:

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, password
  • Shipping Information: Mailing address, phone number
  • Payment Information: Credit card, billing address (processed securely by third-party payment processors)
  • Communications: Emails, messages, customer support inquiries
  • Optional Information: Preferences, feedback, testimonials

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, operating system
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, referral source
  • Cookies: See Cookies section below

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for:

  • Processing and fulfilling orders
  • Sending order confirmations and shipping updates
  • Managing customer service requests and support
  • Sending promotional emails and marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Improving website functionality and user experience
  • Analyzing usage patterns and trends
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Legal compliance and dispute resolution

Data Protection and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure password storage with encryption
  • Limited employee access to personal data
  • Regular security audits and updates

While we strive to protect your information, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Maintain your session and remember preferences
  • Provide personalized content
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Deliver targeted advertising

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may access your information:

Payment Processors

Payment information is processed by secure payment gateways. We do not store full credit card details on our servers. Payment processors comply with PCI-DSS standards.

Analytics

We use analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics) to understand site usage. These services may collect anonymized data about your activity.

Email Marketing

Email communications may be managed through third-party email service providers. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

Shipping Providers

Shipping and fulfillment partners receive your address and contact information necessary for delivery.

User Rights and Choices

Access Your Data

You can request a copy of your personal information by contacting us. We will provide it within 30 days.

Correct or Update Data

You can update your account information by logging in and editing your profile, or by contacting us.

Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your account and associated data. We will retain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

Opt-Out of Marketing

You can unsubscribe from promotional emails by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email or by updating your preferences in your account.

Do Not Track

Some browsers include Do Not Track functionality. We do not currently respond to DNT signals, but honor user preferences where applicable.

Privacy for Minors

Our products are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If we become aware of information collected from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and stored in countries other than your residence. These countries may have data protection laws different from your home country. By using punchDev, you consent to such transfers.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. You can request deletion at any time, subject to legal retention requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes become effective when posted. Your continued use of punchDev following any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page regularly.
