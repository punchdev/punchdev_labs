Shipping & Returns

Last updated: January 2026

Shipping Zones & Timeframes

United States

Processing: 1-2 business days after order confirmation

Delivery: 3-5 business days via USPS/UPS

Cost: $5.99 flat rate (FREE on orders over $50)

International

Processing: 1-2 business days after order confirmation

Delivery: 7-14 business days via USPS International

Cost: Calculated at checkout based on destination

Business days exclude weekends and holidays. These are estimates and may vary due to carrier delays or customs processing.

Order Processing

Orders are processed during business hours (Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM ET). Orders placed outside business hours will be processed the next business day. Once processed, you will receive a shipping confirmation with a tracking number.

Order Tracking

After your order ships, a tracking link will be sent to the email address associated with your account. You can monitor delivery progress through the carrier's tracking system. If you don't receive a tracking number within 24 hours of shipment, contact support.

Delivery Confirmation

punchDev ships all orders with tracking. We recommend choosing a delivery address where someone can receive the package, or requesting a specific delivery time from the carrier.

Lost or Stolen Packages

If your package is marked as delivered but not received, contact support immediately. Include your order number and tracking information. Packages reported missing after 30 days from delivery date cannot be recovered.

Returns Policy

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We stand behind our products. If you're not satisfied for any reason, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on unopened, unused products in original condition.

Return Eligibility

To qualify for a return:

  • Product must be unopened and unused
  • Product must be in original packaging with all seals intact
  • Return must be initiated within 30 days of delivery
  • Proof of purchase (order confirmation) must be provided

How to Initiate a Return

  1. Contact support at support@punchdev.com with your order number
  2. Provide the reason for return and photo of product packaging if applicable
  3. Receive return authorization and shipping instructions
  4. Ship package back to punchDev (address provided with authorization)
  5. Refund processed within 7-10 business days of return receipt

Return Shipping

Customers are responsible for return shipping costs. We recommend using tracked shipping and obtaining proof of delivery. punchDev will not accept returns without prior authorization.

Opened or Used Products

If a product has been opened, used, or shows signs of tampering, it cannot be returned for safety and regulatory reasons. However, if there is a defect or quality issue, contact us immediately for resolution options.

Defective or Damaged Products

Damaged in Shipping

If you receive a damaged product:

  1. Take photos of the damaged product and packaging
  2. Contact support within 48 hours of delivery
  3. We will arrange a replacement or refund immediately

Defective or Expired Products

If you receive a product with a defect, quality issue, or expiration date within 30 days:

  1. Contact support with your order number and photos of the issue
  2. We will send a replacement immediately or process a full refund
  3. Return of the defective product may be requested

Refunds

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 7-10 business days of return receipt. Your bank may take an additional 3-5 business days to reflect the credit. You will receive a confirmation email when your refund is processed.

Refund Status

To check your refund status, contact support with your order number. We can provide updates on received returns and refund processing.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Opened or used products
  • Products without original packaging or seals broken
  • Products returned after 30 days from delivery
  • Packages refused at delivery or left at wrong address

Change of Mind Returns

If you ordered in error or changed your mind, you may return unopened products within 30 days for a refund (minus return shipping costs). Contact support to initiate the return.

Address Changes or Issues

Before Shipment

If you need to change your shipping address, contact support immediately with your order number. We can update the address if the order hasn't been processed yet.

After Shipment

If you need to change the delivery address after shipment, contact the carrier using the tracking link. Some carriers allow address changes within 24 hours of shipment.

International Considerations

Customs and Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties, taxes, or import fees charged by your country. These costs are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in our shipping price.

Restricted Items

Some supplement ingredients may be restricted or prohibited in certain countries. We are not responsible for packages denied by customs. Contact support for clarification before ordering internationally.

Bulk Orders

For bulk orders or wholesale inquiries, contact support. We offer special shipping rates and custom arrangements for larger orders.
