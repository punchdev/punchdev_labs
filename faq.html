Product Questions

Can I stack multiple punchDev products together?

Yes. Most users stack git push with either localhost:3000 or nightly build. Avoid combining git push with merge conflict due to dual caffeine exposure. For custom stacking advice, email support@punchdev.com with your routine and goals.

Do these contain stimulants?

git push contains 200mg caffeine. localhost:3000 and merge conflict are caffeine-free. nightly build contains zero stimulants and is designed for evening use. Check individual product pages for full ingredient lists.

Is this third-party tested?

Yes. All batches undergo third-party testing via NSF Certified for Sport and USP Verified protocols. Testing verifies purity, potency, and absence of banned substances. Certificates are available on each product page.

Will this make me a 10x developer?

Optimized wetware reduces friction and cognitive latency. You'll ship faster and debug more effectively. The remaining 9x depends on your skill, discipline, and sleep. We optimize the biology; you supply the algorithm.

What if I'm already taking other supplements?

In most cases, yes. Our formulations are designed to complement standard vitamin/mineral stacks. Avoid combining git push with other caffeine sources. If you're on prescription medications, consult your doctor before starting any new supplement regimen.

Safety & Usage

Are these safe for long-term use?

Yes. All ingredients are well-researched with established safety profiles for long-term consumption. Daily use is safe. If you have specific health concerns or medical conditions, consult your healthcare provider before starting.

Can I take these if I'm on prescription medications?

Consult your doctor. While most punchDev ingredients are generally safe alongside medications, some may interact. Your doctor can review the ingredient list and advise whether our products are appropriate for your situation.

Are there side effects?

Side effects are rare and usually mild. git push may cause jitters if you're caffeine-sensitive—start with half a scoop. nightly build may cause morning grogginess if taken too late. localhost:3000 and merge conflict are generally well-tolerated. Discontinue if you experience adverse reactions.

How long until I see results?

Focus products (git push): 30-90 minutes. Daily products (localhost:3000): 2-3 weeks. Sleep products (nightly build): 3-5 days. Stress products (merge conflict): 1-2 weeks.

Can pregnant/nursing women take these?

We recommend consulting your OB/GYN before taking any supplements while pregnant or nursing. While many of our ingredients are food-derived, pregnancy and lactation have unique nutritional needs. Your healthcare provider can assess safety based on your individual situation.
