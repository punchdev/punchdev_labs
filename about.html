The Origin Story

Four years ago, I architected systems. Today, I architect the nervous system. The catalyst? A brutal crash after 72 hours of Red Bull and espresso chasing a production outage.

I spent the next six months reading peer-reviewed neuroscience. Not bro-science, not YouTube testimonials—actual papers from JAMA, Nutrients, and the American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. I learned that most supplements are either placebos or spiked with crap that makes your heart feel like it's compiling a massive codebase.

So I built something different. A supplement stack with the same rigor I'd apply to production infrastructure. Transparent dosing. Evidence-based ingredients. No proprietary blends hiding behind marketing fluff.

Three months later, I shipped it to friends. Now punchDev serves developers who refuse to compromise on either their code or their wetware.

Our Philosophy

If you can't measure it, you can't optimize it

Every ingredient in punchDev ships with its dosage clearly labeled. No proprietary blends, no secret sauce. We publish our formulations because hiding behind mystery ingredients is how legacy supplement companies operate, and we're not legacy.

Evidence beats hype

Every ingredient has peer-reviewed research. Not testimonials, not anecdotes—actual studies showing mechanism and efficacy. We cite them. You can read them. This is non-negotiable.

Third-party testing is mandatory

Every batch gets tested by independent labs for purity, potency, and heavy metals. The certs are public. If we find heavy metals, we bin the batch. No asterisks, no excuses.

Why We're Different

Nootropic Brands

We publish everything. Dosages, research, testing certs. This is how you build trust with engineers.
