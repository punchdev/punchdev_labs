Rhodiola Rosea (400mg)

Scandinavian adaptogen for fatigue resilience. Prevents stress from collapsing into exhaustion. Used by military and athletes for sustained performance under adversity. Sharpens cognitive function while reducing perceived effort.

Mechanism: Increases serotonin and dopamine availability while stabilizing cortisol patterns. Reduces mental fatigue during sustained cognitive load (long debugging sessions, incident response). Does not sedate.

Mineral co-factor in 300+ enzymatic reactions. Glycinate form crosses blood-brain barrier efficiently and doesn't cause laxative effects (unlike magnesium oxide). Activates parasympathetic nervous system. Calms without sedation.

Mechanism: NMDA receptor antagonist (blocks excitotoxicity). Facilitates relaxation response. Improves sleep quality without making you groggy during day. Synergizes with ashwagandha for anxiety relief.

The Science

Developer stress isn't in your head—it's in your HPA axis. Chronic deadline pressure, incident response, and imposter syndrome dysregulate cortisol. merge conflict is formulated to restore that dysregulation with evidence-backed adaptogens.

Chandrasekhar et al. (2012) - Journal of Clinical Psychiatry

Ashwagandha KSM-66 (600mg daily) reduced cortisol by 26% and anxiety scores by 44% over 60 days vs placebo. Participants reported improved focus, sleep quality, and resilience to stress. No tolerability issues.

Study: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled (n=64). Industry-standard for ashwagandha efficacy.

Darbinyan et al. (2000) - Phytomedicine

Rhodiola Rosea (400mg daily) reduced mental fatigue and improved task performance under sustained cognitive demand. Cortisol levels normalized during stress without sedation. Participants worked longer before exhaustion set in.

Study: Double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover (n=56). Swedish military research.

Boyle et al. (2017) - Nutrients

Magnesium glycinate improved anxiety scores and sleep quality without sedation during day. Particularly effective for individuals with chronic stress and poor sleep. Synergistic effects when combined with other adaptogens.

Study: Meta-analysis of 18 clinical trials. Glycinate form superior to oxide for nervous system effects.
