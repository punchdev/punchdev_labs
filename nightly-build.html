The Science

Why these three ingredients beat melatonin supplementation for developers.

1 Why Tart Cherry?

Tart cherry naturally produces melatonin—25 mg per 500 mg serving—but delivers it alongside polyphenols that regulate circadian entrainment. You're not supplementing melatonin externally; you're restoring your body's native production pathway.

Studies show tart cherry reduces sleep onset latency by 16 minutes on average and increases total sleep time by 39 minutes. No next-morning grogginess because your body's still running its own circadian rhythm.

Howatson et al. (2012): Cherry juice improves sleep quality in athletes. J Int Soc Sports Nutr.

2 Why Apigenin?

Apigenin is a benzodiazepine-like compound that binds GABA receptors—but without the addiction risk, tolerance buildup, or next-day cognitive fog. It's the pharmaceutical shortcut without the long-term liability.

At 50 mg, apigenin reduces sleep latency and increases Stage N3 (deep sleep) without suppressing REM architecture. You consolidate procedural memory (debugging patterns, API flows) alongside declarative memory (facts and concepts).

Zanoli et al. (2000): Apigenin modulates GABA receptors in vivo. Neurosci Lett.

3 Why L-Glycine?

Glycine lowers core body temperature by 0.5–1.0°C through vasodilation. Cooler core = longer REM episodes = better memory consolidation. It's the thermostat for your brain.

At 3 g, L-glycine increases total sleep time by ~25 minutes on average and improves sleep continuity. No receptor tolerance. Zero morning residue. Pure biological optimization.

Bannai et al. (2012): Glycine promotes sleep quality via temperature reduction. Sleep Biol Rhythms.

4 Why NOT Melatonin?

Exogenous melatonin works fine for jet lag—acute circadian disruption—but chronic supplementation dysregulates your endogenous production. Your pineal gland outsources and atrophies.

Developers report grogginess, brain fog, and emotional blunting from high-dose melatonin. nightly build activates your body's native pathways. You wake sharp because you optimized the entire sleep architecture, not just sleep initiation.

You are not a passenger on melatonin. You are the architect of your own recovery.
