What's Inside

Caffeine (200mg)

Adenosine receptor antagonist. Blocks fatigue signals, increases dopamine and norepinephrine. Equivalent to ~2 cups of coffee.

Mechanism: Competitive antagonism at adenosine A1/A2A receptors → increased alertness, reduced perceived effort during cognitive tasks.

L-Theanine (400mg)

Amino acid from green tea. Increases alpha brain waves (relaxed focus). Prevents caffeine jitters. 1:2 ratio with caffeine is the studied sweet spot.

Mechanism: Modulates GABA, serotonin, dopamine. Crosses blood-brain barrier. Synergistic with caffeine for attention and task-switching.

Alpha GPC (300mg)

Choline source that crosses the blood-brain barrier. Precursor to acetylcholine (learning and memory neurotransmitter). Prevents "brain fog" during extended focus sessions.

Mechanism: Increases acetylcholine synthesis → enhanced working memory, faster processing speed, reduced mental fatigue.

When to Take It

Optimal Timing

  • Pre-deep work: Take 30-45 minutes before coding sessions for peak effect during flow state.
  • Morning/early afternoon: Avoid after 2pm if caffeine-sensitive (6hr half-life means 100mg still in system at bedtime).
  • Empty stomach: Faster absorption. If nausea occurs, take with small snack.

Stacking Recommendations

✓ Stack with: localhost:3000 (daily vitamins), nightly build (for sleep recovery)

The Science

The caffeine + L-theanine combination isn't bro science. It's one of the most well-studied nootropic stacks with peer-reviewed evidence for cognitive enhancement.

Haskell et al. (2008) - Nutritional Neuroscience

Combined caffeine (97mg) + L-theanine (40mg) improved accuracy during task-switching and reduced susceptibility to distracting information compared to placebo. Subjective alertness increased without jitteriness.

Study: Double-blind, placebo-controlled, crossover design (n=27)

Giesbrecht et al. (2010) - Appetite

L-theanine counteracted caffeine-induced increases in blood pressure while preserving caffeine's beneficial effects on attention. 1:2 ratio (50mg caffeine, 100mg L-theanine) optimal for "alert calmness."

Study: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled (n=44)

Ziegenfuss et al. (2015) - J. Int. Soc. Sports Nutr.

Alpha-GPC (600mg) increased peak bench press force and power output. Cognitive benefits include faster reaction time and improved working memory during mentally demanding tasks.

Study: Randomized, placebo-controlled, crossover (n=13)
