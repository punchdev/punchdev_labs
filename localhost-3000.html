The Science

Vitamin D deficiency in office workers isn't speculative—it's epidemiological fact. B-vitamin inadequacy impairs cognitive function. Magnesium deficiency is rampant in Western diets. localhost:3000 addresses documented nutritional gaps.

Tsai et al. (2011) - BMC Public Health

Study of Taiwanese office workers found 90% had insufficient vitamin D levels (<30 ng/mL). Indoor workers showed significantly lower D3 compared to outdoor workers. Deficiency correlated with fatigue and reduced work performance.

Study: Cross-sectional analysis (n=1,346 office workers)

Boyle et al. (2017) - Nutrients

Magnesium supplementation (300-400mg daily) reduced subjective anxiety scores in mildly anxious adults. Mechanism involves modulation of HPA axis and neurotransmitter regulation. Glycinate form showed superior absorption.

Study: Systematic review of 18 studies

Kennedy et al. (2016) - Human Psychopharmacology

High-dose B-vitamin supplementation (B6, B9, B12) improved stress-related mood and cognitive performance in healthy adults during chronic work-related stress. Methylated forms bypass genetic polymorphisms affecting B-vitamin metabolism.

Study: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled (n=215)
